# Sahatna Super App Overview

The Sahatna Super App is a Digital Front Door (DFD) healthcare application developed by Core42 for the Abu Dhabi Department of Health (DOH). Version 1.3, dated 11.05.2025.

## Purpose
Provides unified access to healthcare services for UAE residents including:
- Appointment booking and management
- Teleconsultation (video conferencing)
- Patient Health Records (PHR) access
- Symptoms checking with AI
- Wearables and wellness data integration
- AI-powered chatbot assistance
- Genomics data access
- Payment processing

## Target Users
- Patients (UAE residents and citizens)
- Healthcare providers (physicians, facilities)
- Healthcare administrators

## Key Stakeholders
- Abu Dhabi Department of Health (DOH)
- Core42 (developer)
- M42 (AI/genomics partner)
- Healthcare facilities across Abu Dhabi
