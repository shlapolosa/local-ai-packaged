# Genomics Service

## Overview
Responsible for getting Patient Genome Records from Genomics database handled by M42.

## Main Features
- Retrieval of patient genome records
- Integration with M42 genomics platforms

## M42 Genomics Platforms

### Emirati Genome Programme (EGP)
A national project which aims to use genomic data to improve the health of the Emirati population.

### Gensense by M42
The platform provides personalized health insights through comprehensive genetic analysis, allowing individuals to gain a deeper understanding of their genetic predispositions, health risks, and microbiome composition from the convenience of their home.
