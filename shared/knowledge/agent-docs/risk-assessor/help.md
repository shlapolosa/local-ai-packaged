# Risk Assessor Agent

## What I Do
I analyze project and architectural risks, create risk registers, and provide mitigation strategies. I follow industry frameworks for comprehensive risk assessment.

## Commands
- `risk:help` - Show this help
- `risk:status` - Check my knowledge status
- `risk:list` - List my knowledge collections
- `risk:query <question>` - Ask me about risk management, threat modeling, or mitigation
- `risk:upload` - Add knowledge to my collection (attach a file)
- `risk:reload` - Refresh my knowledge from source files

## Examples
- "Assess risks for a healthcare data platform" - Full risk analysis
- "risk:query what are common API security risks?"
- "risk:query how do I create a risk matrix?"
- "risk:upload" + attach risk frameworks or assessment templates

## My Knowledge Topics
- Risk identification techniques
- Risk assessment methodologies
- TOGAF risk catalog
- Threat modeling (STRIDE, DREAD)
- Risk matrices and scoring
- Mitigation strategies
- Business continuity planning
- Compliance risk assessment
- Vendor risk management
- Operational risk management

## Output Artifacts
When I execute a risk assessment, I produce:
- Risk Assessment Report (Markdown)
- Risk Register (with likelihood/impact scores)
- Mitigation recommendations
- Risk matrix visualization
- Compliance gap analysis

## Risk Categories
I assess risks across multiple dimensions:
- **Technical risks**: Architecture, technology choices
- **Security risks**: Threats, vulnerabilities
- **Operational risks**: Process, people, systems
- **Compliance risks**: Regulatory requirements
- **Business risks**: Market, financial, strategic

## Integration
I work with other agents in the architecture pipeline:
- I analyze risks from **Architect** designs
- I review **Solution Architect** API security
- **Project Manager** incorporates my findings in planning
- **Test Strategist** uses risks to prioritize testing
