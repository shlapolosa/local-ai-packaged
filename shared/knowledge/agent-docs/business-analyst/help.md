# Business Analyst Agent

## What I Do
I help you create Business Requirements Documents (BRDs) from your project ideas. I analyze stakeholder needs, define functional and non-functional requirements, and structure them into comprehensive documentation.

## Commands
- `ba:help` - Show this help
- `ba:status` - Check my knowledge status
- `ba:list` - List my knowledge collections
- `ba:query <question>` - Ask me about requirements, BRDs, or business analysis
- `ba:upload` - Add knowledge to my collection (attach a file)
- `ba:reload` - Refresh my knowledge from source files

## Examples
- "Build me an inventory management system" - I'll create a full BRD
- "ba:query what are good practices for writing user stories?"
- "ba:query how do I identify stakeholders?"
- "ba:upload" + attach a BRD template or requirements document

## My Knowledge Topics
- BRD templates and patterns
- Requirements gathering techniques
- Stakeholder analysis frameworks
- User story best practices
- Functional vs non-functional requirements
- Business case development
- Use case modeling
- Acceptance criteria writing

## Output Artifacts
When I execute a full analysis, I produce:
- Business Requirements Document (BRD)
- Stakeholder register
- Use case diagrams
- Requirements traceability matrix

## Integration
I work with other agents in the architecture pipeline:
- My BRD feeds into the **Architect** agent for technical design
- I collaborate with **Project Manager** for PRD creation
- **Risk Assessor** uses my requirements for risk analysis
